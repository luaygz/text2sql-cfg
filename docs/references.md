## Details corrected while checking the sources

Checking each reference against its source corrected these details; the entries above follow the sources:

- Tam et al. [8]: the EMNLP Industry Track title differs from the arXiv title.
- MCS-SQL [13]: published at COLING 2025; the arXiv preprint is from 2024. Official capitalisation is "For Text-to-SQL Generation".
- CHESS [12]: never renamed; v3 (Nov 2024) rewrote the method as four agents including the Unit Tester. Only venue appearance found is an ICML 2025 workshop poster.
- Maamari et al. [15]: NeurIPS 2024 TRL workshop paper (oral), not a main-conference paper.
- XiYan-SQL [16, 17]: arXiv:2411.08599 is now titled "A Preview of XiYan-SQL: …"; the full version arXiv:2507.04701 has a different title, first author Yifu Liu (not Gao), and is published in IEEE TKDE 2026.
- OpenSearch-SQL [18]: authors Xie, Xu, Zhao, Guo; published in PACMMOD 3(3), 2025.
- Pervasive Annotation Errors [20]: published in PVLDB 19(5), 2026.
- ReViSQL [21]: "ReViSQL-BIRD" is a method name, not part of the title; the title changed between v2 and v3 and so did the headline numbers. Still a preprint.
- Pedro et al. [24]: published at ICSE 2025 under a new title with an added author.
- Self-consistency [22] first appeared on arXiv in 2022; ICLR 2023 is the venue year.
